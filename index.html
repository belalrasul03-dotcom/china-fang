Our Story

More Than a Meal —
It's an Experience

Nestled in the heart of Murphy, Texas, China Fang brings together the bold, time-honored flavors of Chinese cooking with the delicate artistry of pan-Asian cuisine and fresh, hand-crafted sushi. We believe great food is about more than just ingredients — it's about the care and craft that goes into every single dish.

Whether you're stopping in for a quick weekday lunch, gathering with family for a weekend dinner, or treating yourself to a full sushi spread, our kitchen is ready to make it memorable. From fragrant stir-fries and steaming soups to perfectly rolled maki, everything is made fresh, every day.
